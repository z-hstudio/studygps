## Block 1 — energy balance (30 minutes)

For these closed-system exercises, ignore changes in kinetic and potential energy. Use:

```text
change in internal energy = heat added to the system − work done by the system
ΔU = Q − W
```

Heat entering the system is positive `Q`; work leaving the system is positive `W`. Keep all quantities in kJ.

1. A system receives 120 kJ of heat and does 45 kJ of work. Calculate `ΔU`.
2. A system loses 30 kJ of heat while 20 kJ of work is done on it. Write the signed values of `Q` and `W`, then calculate `ΔU`.
3. Explain in one sentence why work done **on** the system has a negative `W` under this sign convention.

## Block 2 — rearrange and check (30 minutes)

1. The internal energy rises by 50 kJ and the system does 15 kJ of work. Calculate the heat added.
2. With no heat transfer, 25 kJ of work is done on the system. Calculate the internal-energy change.
3. Write your own example with `Q = W`. Calculate `ΔU` and explain what the balance says about internal energy.
4. Check each result by substituting it back into `ΔU = Q − W`.

## Worked checks

- Block 1.1: `120 − 45 = 75 kJ`.
- Block 1.2: `Q = −30 kJ`, `W = −20 kJ`; `ΔU = −30 − (−20) = −10 kJ`.
- Block 1.3: This convention counts work leaving as positive, so work entering is negative.
- Block 2.1: `Q = ΔU + W = 50 + 15 = 65 kJ`.
- Block 2.2: `Q = 0`, `W = −25 kJ`; `ΔU = 0 − (−25) = 25 kJ`.
- Block 2.3: For example, `Q = W = 10 kJ`, so `ΔU = 0 kJ`: no net internal-energy change in this balance.
